About

I photograph places the way they feel at the edges of the day: before the light arrives and after most people have gone home.

For the past ten years I have worked on long-term projects in Iceland, Norway and the American Southwest, often returning to the same location for several seasons. My work has been published by National Geographic Traveller, Kinfolk and Lonely Planet, and is held in private collections in 14 countries.

Alongside my own projects, I shoot for tourism boards, outdoor brands and hotels who want images that feel honest rather than staged.
