Questions

How do my clients pay?

Every invoice includes a secure payment link. Clients can pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or bank transfer, without creating an account.

Are there transaction fees?

Card payments cost 1.4% + €0.25, the same as the payment processor charges us. Bank transfers are free.

Can I use my own logo and colours?

Yes. On the Freelancer and Studio plans your invoices carry only your branding.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. There are no contracts. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your billing period and can export all your data.
