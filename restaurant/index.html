One fire, one kitchen,
nothing frozen.

Brace started in 2012 as a twelve-seat counter around a single oak-burning oven. The oven is still the heart of the kitchen. Everything that leaves it, from the bread to the bistecca, picks up a little of the smoke.

We buy from farms within a day's drive, change the menu when the season does, and roll every sheet of pasta by hand before lunch.
